Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a word in the puzzle?

Words are hidden horizontally, vertically, and diagonally — including backwards. Tap the first letter and drag to the last to highlight your selection.

How do I turn off daily word notifications?

Go to your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Lexisynap and turn them off there. You can also adjust the notification time from within the app.

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Puzzle progress is saved locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, progress will have been reset. We're sorry — there's no cloud backup at this stage.
