My progress disappeared

Your quiz scores and achievements are saved locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, this data is reset. Progress is not backed up to iCloud or any server — it lives on your device only.

A country or fact seems wrong

We've done our best to make every question accurate, but geography changes and mistakes happen. If you spot something incorrect, please let us know at abbyfitz2@icloud.com and we'll fix it in the next update.

The globe isn't loading

Geosynap works entirely offline — no internet connection is needed. If the globe isn't appearing, try closing the app fully and reopening it. If the problem continues, please get in touch.

I want to suggest a feature

We'd love to hear from you. Send a note to abbyfitz2@icloud.com with your idea.
