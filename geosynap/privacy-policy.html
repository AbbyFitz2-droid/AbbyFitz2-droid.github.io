Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Geosynap is a geography quiz app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version: Geosynap collects no personal data, creates no accounts, and shares nothing with anyone.

What We Collect

Nothing. Geosynap does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no data ever leaves your device.

What Stays on Your Device

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device only, using iOS standard storage. This includes:

  • Your quiz progress and scores for each country
  • Achievements you have earned
  • Your preferred quiz mode (Expert, Flag Quiz, or Capitals)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Third Parties

Geosynap contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party services of any kind. The app works entirely offline. Your usage of the app is entirely private.

Children

Geosynap does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is appropriate for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
